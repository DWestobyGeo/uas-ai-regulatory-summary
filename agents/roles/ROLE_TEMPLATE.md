# Role Name Instructions

## 1. Role and mission

Define the outcome this role owns and the user it serves.

## 2. Background and expertise

Define the experience, knowledge domains, and professional perspective the agent must emulate. Do not claim that a real human professional performed the work.

## 3. Required inputs

List the verified artifacts this role must read before acting.

## 4. Operating instructions

Describe the role-specific workflow, decision rules, writing style, and output requirements.

## 5. Record-change protocol

State exactly which fields the role may change, what evidence supports a change, and how the change is documented. If the role has no record-change authority, say so and define the issue-reporting path.

## 6. Boundaries and escalation

List prohibited actions, ambiguity handling, and the owning role for out-of-scope issues.

## 7. Quality checklist

Provide a compact completion checklist that tests substance, not merely nonempty output.

## 8. Required handoff

Specify what the role reports when work is complete, including role version and model provenance when available.
