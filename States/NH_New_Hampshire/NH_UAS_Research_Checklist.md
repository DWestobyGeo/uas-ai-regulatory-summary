# New Hampshire UAS Research Checklist

- **State:** New Hampshire (NH)
- **Research date:** 2026-08-02
- **Phase:** Phase 1 — Objective Research
- **Scope:** Statewide and state-agency UAS authorities only. Municipal, county, tribal, federal, and site-specific local layers are outside this pass.

| # | Coverage category | Status | Research note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | State statutes and amendments | Applicable sources found | RSA 422 and RSA 644:23 were reviewed with 2025 chapter 278, the 2026 chapter 6 amendment, RSA 207:57, RSA 430:34-a, and 2026 chapter 95. The 2026 chapter laws were checked because the online RSA compilation is current only through December 2025. |
| 2 | Administrative rules and regulations | Applicable sources found | Tra 903, Fis 301/312, Fis 900, Fis 2100, and Ncr 700 were searched and the direct UAS provisions recorded. General pesticide rules remain relevant in addition to the UAS-specific statutory exception. |
| 3 | Executive orders | Reviewed — no separate current commercial-operator requirement located | Current gubernatorial executive-order and directive materials were searched. No enduring UAS-specific general commercial-flight restriction was located. |
| 4 | Court decisions and attorney-general materials | Reviewed — no controlling UAS-specific construction located | New Hampshire court and Department of Justice materials were searched for UAS, drone, RSA 644:23, privacy, and aeronautics interpretations. No controlling published decision or Attorney General directive construing the current UAS provisions was located. |
| 5 | Aviation and transportation agencies | Applicable statutory framework found; implementation question remains | RSA 422 and Tra 903 create aircraft registration, identification, fee, impairment, and enforcement rules. Their definitions appear to reach federally registered UAS, but no UAS-specific NHDOT registration instructions, form, exemption, or enforcement statement was located. NHDOT program reports were treated as context, not operator requirements. |
| 6 | Parks, public lands, forestry, fish, wildlife, and natural resources | Applicable sources found | Fis 312 regulates UAS-assisted taking and wildlife location; RSA 207:57 and Fis 2101 regulate interference and surveillance; Fis 902 requires a special-use permit on Fish and Game lands; Ncr 731.33 requires a permit or contract on DNCR reservations. |
| 7 | Corrections, public safety, emergency management, and critical infrastructure | Applicable sources found | RSA 644:23 governs emergency-response interference, human-occupied aircraft and airport interference, FAA-restricted airspace over correctional facilities, and weaponized or projectile-capable small UAS. No enacted separate general critical-infrastructure buffer was located. |
| 8 | Privacy, surveillance, harassment, trespass, and interference | Applicable UAS-specific sources found | RSA 644:23 expressly incorporates RSA 644:9 and separately reaches emergency interference. Hunter surveillance and institutional privacy controls are recorded. A 2026 private-property surveillance proposal did not become current law and was not represented as binding. |
| 9 | Procurement, equipment, cybersecurity, and manufacturer restrictions | Applicable prospective procurement law found | 2026 chapter 95 took effect July 27, 2026 and establishes government-acquisition preferences and prohibitions, principally operative January 1, 2028. It does not establish a blanket ban on private purchase, possession, or operation. |
| 10 | Professional licensing-board materials | Pesticide specialty found; no separate UAS engineering or surveying rule located | Professional engineering, land-surveying, photogrammetry, mapping, and licensing-board materials were searched. No UAS-specific professional licensure rule was located. RSA 430:34-a is included because it expressly governs and partially excepts agricultural unmanned-aircraft pesticide application. |
| 11 | State preemption and local authority | Reviewed — no express blanket UAS preemption located | Current statutes and enacted UAS chapter laws were searched for a statewide local-preemption clause. None was located. This does not validate any particular local rule and does not expand this pass into municipal or county research. |

## Open verification items

1. Obtain written NHDOT confirmation of whether and how RSA 422 aircraft registration, identifier display, waiver, and operating-fee provisions apply to the specific FAA-registered UAS and owner residency.
2. Confirm that mission planning uses the May 19, 2026 chapter 6 version of RSA 644:23(II), not only the online RSA page that remains current through December 2025.
3. Map airports, crewed-aircraft activity, emergency-response operations, and correctional-facility FAA restrictions around the site and document immediate deconfliction procedures.
4. Build a sensor and privacy plan for dwellings, windows, audio, people, thermal imagery, derived data, retention, and release under RSA 644:9.
5. Check Fish and Game seasons, wildlife sensitivity, active hunting/fishing/trapping, the current Department-controlled-land list, and written-consent needs for participant surveillance.
6. For Fish and Game or DNCR property, obtain the current application, property map, lead time, fees, insurance, agency contacts, permit or contract, and all site-specific operating conditions.
7. For pesticide dispensing, confirm the proposed platform, applicator and business credentials, agricultural purpose, altitude, label, approval, notice, buffer, insurance, and record requirements with the Division.
8. For UNH or government-client work, confirm current institutional approvals and equipment eligibility, including how 2026 chapter 95 will be implemented before and after January 1, 2028.
