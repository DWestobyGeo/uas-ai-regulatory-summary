# South Carolina UAS Research Checklist

- **State:** South Carolina (SC)
- **Research date:** 2026-08-02
- **Phase:** Phase 1 — Objective Research
- **Scope:** Statewide and state-agency UAS authorities only. Municipal, county, tribal, federal, and site-specific local layers are outside this pass.

| # | Coverage category | Status | Research note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | State statutes and amendments | Applicable source found | Current correctional statutes and enacted 2026 Act No. 150 were reviewed. The register distinguishes requirements in force through December 31, 2026 from the new Article 3 requirements effective January 1, 2027. Proposed bills that did not enact were excluded. |

## Open verification items

1. For work before January 1, 2027 near a correctional or detention facility, use the current 500-foot horizontal and 250-foot vertical rule and document either written consent or every element of the infrastructure notification exception.
2. For work on or after January 1, 2027, remap the project against the new 1,500-foot horizontal correctional and military property-boundary buffers and obtain the required express written consent; do not assume the former infrastructure notice exception remains available.
3. Check the Aeronautics Commission SCDOC boundary map and current FAA fixed-site data before each mission, and resolve any disagreement between map data, property information, and the governing statutory measurement with the facility authority.
4. For state-park work, contact the park and permit contact early enough to obtain location-specific conditions and provide the required insurance certificate and additional-insured documentation.
5. For surveying, LiDAR, contours, or overlapping-image photogrammetry, confirm the professional scope, responsible licensee, seals, supervision, and deliverable classification under the current Board rules and policy.
6. For federally funded SCDOT or local public agency work, verify the current FASC covered-entity list, the applicable cleared list, the full UAS and processing supply chain, and every project-specific flow-down clause before assigning equipment.
