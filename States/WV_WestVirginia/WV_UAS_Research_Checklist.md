# West Virginia UAS Research Checklist

- **State:** West Virginia (WV)
- **Research date:** 2026-08-02
- **Phase:** Phase 1 — Objective Research
- **Scope:** Statewide and state-agency UAS authorities only. Municipal, county, tribal, federal, and site-specific local layers are outside this pass.

| # | Coverage category | Status | Research note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | State statutes and amendments | Applicable source found | Current Chapters 5B, 15, 20, and 61 and the 2025–26 sessions were reviewed. Applicable UAS provisions are included. The 2026 procurement bill (HB 5552), polling-place bill (HB 5225), and correctional-targeted-facility bill (SB 900) were not enacted and are not stated as current requirements. |
| 2 | Administrative rules and regulations | Reviewed — no additional applicable UAS-specific source located | Current state materials did not reveal a separate generally applicable UAS administrative rule beyond the statutory park-registration, wildlife, and outfitter/guide provisions included in the register. Mission-specific agency contract terms may still apply. |
| 3 | Executive orders | Reviewed — no applicable UAS-specific source located | The current governor's executive-order index and prior executive-order materials were searched. No current operator-facing UAS executive order or statewide UAS procurement restriction was located. |
| 4 | Court decisions and attorney-general materials | Reviewed — no applicable UAS-specific source located | Current Supreme Court of Appeals, Intermediate Court of Appeals, and attorney-general opinion materials were searched. No controlling West Virginia appellate decision or formal attorney-general opinion specifically applying state law to ordinary commercial UAS operations was located. A March 2026 attorney-general press release on prison counter-UAS authority is advocacy, not an operative operator rule. |
| 5 | Aviation and transportation agencies | Applicable source found | W. Va. Code 5B-2-18a permits federally compliant UAS operations and preempts most political-subdivision regulation. No separate state commercial UAS registration, pilot credential, or transportation-agency permit was located. |
| 6 | Parks, public lands, forestry, fish, wildlife, and natural resources | Applicable source found | W. Va. Code 20-5-2(16) requires advance registration with the area superintendent for state parks, forests, and rail trails. Sections 20-2-5 and 20-2-5j govern UAS use involving wildlife, including the 2025 wounded-game recovery procedure. |
| 7 | Corrections, public safety, emergency management, and critical infrastructure | Applicable source found | Article 61-16 prohibits specified malicious UAS activity over targeted critical-infrastructure facilities and interference with law-enforcement or emergency-medical duties. No enacted UAS-specific correctional overflight provision was located; 2026 SB 900 remained pending at concurrence. General contraband laws were not entered absent an official UAS-specific application. |
| 8 | Privacy, surveillance, harassment, trespass, and interference | Applicable source found | W. Va. Code 61-16-2 prohibits intentional privacy invasion, harassment, restraining-order violations, wanton safety disregard, and emergency-personnel interference by UAS. It also recognizes operations over owned, leased, easement, right-of-way, permitted, licensed, or otherwise authorized property and owner-retained third-party imaging. |
| 9 | Procurement, equipment, cybersecurity, and manufacturer restrictions | Reviewed — no applicable current UAS-specific source located | No enacted statewide restriction on commercial UAS manufacturers, country of origin, or equipment configuration was located. 2026 HB 5552 proposed restrictions for public entities and state funds but remained pending in House Judiciary and is not current law. Contract-specific client requirements remain possible. |
| 10 | Professional licensing-board materials | Reviewed — no applicable UAS-specific source located | West Virginia professional engineering and surveying board materials were searched; no UAS-specific board rule, opinion, or bulletin was located. General professional-practice and survey-certification duties remain outside the register absent an official UAS application. |
| 11 | State preemption | Applicable source found | W. Va. Code 5B-2-18a voids political-subdivision ordinances, policies, or rules regulating UAS ownership or operation except as otherwise authorized by law. |

## Open verification items

1. Before a state park, forest, or rail-trail mission, obtain the superintendent's current registration procedure and the required prohibited-area list and map; the statute does not prescribe a statewide form or minimum lead time.
2. For a mission involving a “targeted facility,” confirm the current facility classification under W. Va. Code 61-10-34 and document the property right, owner authorization, purpose, payload, and data-handling scope.
3. Confirm whether the Division of Economic Development has published the UAS promotion report or recommendations required by the current version of W. Va. Code 5B-2-20 and whether any recommendation has since become operative law.
4. For paid wounded-game recovery, confirm the current outfitter/guide licensing process, the DNR's annual public list, the hunting-license status of all required participants, and any current-year wildlife rules.
5. Recheck the next legislative update for any enacted successor to 2026 HB 5552, HB 5225, or SB 900; none was operative on the research date.
