# Wyoming UAS Research Checklist

- **State:** Wyoming (WY)
- **Research date:** 2026-08-02
- **Phase:** Phase 1 — Objective Research
- **Scope:** Statewide and state-agency UAS authorities only. Municipal, county, tribal, federal, and site-specific local layers are outside this pass.

| # | Coverage category | Status | Research note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | State statutes and amendments | Applicable source found | Current Titles 6, 10, 19, 23, and the 2023/2025 session materials were reviewed. Applicable UAS provisions are included in the source register. The 2026 Budget Session was checked; no enacted UAS amendment was located. |
| 2 | Administrative rules and regulations | Applicable source found | Current Wyoming Game and Fish Commission Chapter 2 expressly defines aircraft to include UAVs and restricts aircraft-assisted game spotting. No separate current Aeronautics Commission small-UAS rule was located. |
| 3 | Executive orders | Reviewed — no applicable UAS-specific source located | Searches of current governor and state materials did not locate a Wyoming executive order imposing an operator-facing UAS requirement. |
| 4 | Court decisions and attorney-general materials | Reviewed — no applicable UAS-specific source located | No controlling Wyoming appellate decision or attorney-general opinion specifically applying state law to UAS operations was located. An official Legislative Service Office memorandum discussing unsettled aerial trespass and privacy issues is included as nonbinding guidance. |
| 5 | Aviation and transportation agencies | Applicable source found | Title 10 contains a large-UAS aeronautics framework and accident-reporting rule, but its definition of “unmanned aircraft” expressly excludes federally defined small unmanned aircraft. WYDOT's commercial-operator page adds no located state permit or registration requirement. |
| 6 | Parks, public lands, forestry, fish, wildlife, and natural resources | Unresolved — additional verification required | The Game and Fish statute and Chapter 2 rule are included. State Parks Chapter 1, Section 6 regulates “aircraft” landing/use and certain air delivery, but no official source located in this pass confirmed that State Parks applies that term to UAS; the rule was therefore not entered as a UAS authority. |
| 7 | Corrections, public safety, emergency management, and critical infrastructure | Applicable source found | W.S. 6-5-214, WDOC's Form 366 request channel, and W.S. 19-13-501/503 are included. |
| 8 | Privacy, surveillance, harassment, trespass, and interference | Applicable source found | The 2022 LSO UAS memorandum identifies possible common-law intrusion-upon-seclusion and aerial-trespass theories but describes small-UAS trespass law as unsettled. No enacted small-UAS private-property overflight statute was located in the current code. |
| 9 | Procurement, equipment, cybersecurity, and manufacturer restrictions | Reviewed — no applicable UAS-specific source located | No Wyoming law, executive order, or statewide procurement policy restricting a commercial consultant's UAS manufacturer, country of origin, or equipment configuration was located. Counter-UAS authority in W.S. 19-13-503 is operational/enforcement authority, not a fleet-purchasing restriction. |
| 10 | Professional licensing-board materials | Reviewed — no applicable UAS-specific source located | The Wyoming Board of Professional Engineers and Professional Land Surveyors website, rules page, and current materials were searched; no UAS-specific board rule, opinion, or bulletin was located. General professional-practice requirements remain outside the source register absent an official UAS application. |
| 11 | State preemption | Reviewed — no applicable UAS-specific source located | No Wyoming statute expressly preempting municipal or county UAS regulation was located. W.S. 10-3-201(j) requires Aeronautics Commission coordination with political subdivisions but is not an express local-preemption clause. |
