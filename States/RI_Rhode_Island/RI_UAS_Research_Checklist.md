# Rhode Island UAS Research Checklist

- **State:** Rhode Island (RI)
- **Research date:** 2026-08-02
- **Phase:** Phase 1 — Objective Research
- **Scope:** Statewide and state-agency UAS authorities only. Municipal, county, tribal, federal, and site-specific local layers are outside this pass.

| # | Coverage category | Status | Research note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | State statutes and amendments | Applicable source found | Current Title 1 aeronautics provisions and 2026 session materials were reviewed. Sections 1-8-1.1 and 1-8-1.2 establish the current statewide UAS rule and preemption boundary; § 1-4-6 supplies the state aircraft-registration framework. No enacted 2026 UAS amendment was located. |
| 2 | Administrative rules and regulations | Applicable source found | The active RIAC Aeronautics Regulations and current RIDEM public-reservation rule are included. Superseded RIAC UAS provisions were excluded after confirming the January 29, 2025 amendment. |

No controlling state decision or formal opinion directly applying the current UAS provisions to ordinary commercial AEC operations was located. |

| 7 | Corrections, public safety, emergency management, and critical infrastructure | Reviewed — no applicable UAS-specific source located | Current statutes, agency rules, and official materials were searched. | 9 | Procurement, equipment, cybersecurity, and manufacturer restrictions | Reviewed — no applicable UAS-specific source located | State procurement and security materials were searched. ## Open verification items

1. Before registering a Part 107 fleet, confirm with RIAC how § 1-4-6 and 800-RICR-10-00-1 § 1.9 apply to small UAS whose FAA documents and identifiers do not match the conventional-aircraft fields in the published application.
2. Confirm whether RIAC treats any general all-aircraft provisions outside the UAS-specific § 1.17 as applicable to the planned small-UAS operation, particularly if the mission uses RIAC airport property or facilities.
3. For a RIDEM public reservation, identify the property manager, obtain official written permission, and complete the commercial special-use process early enough for the 30-business-day minimum or any longer license-agreement review.
4. Confirm the park-specific operating conditions, insurance amount, certificate submission instructions, impact costs, visitor controls, and resource-protection limits before mobilization.
